# Security

This platform holds sensitive personal data (church membership reveals religious affiliation).

**Report a vulnerability or a suspected data leak privately** through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository, or by contacting the technical lead directly. Do not open a public issue, and do not include members' personal details in any report.

Personal-data breaches must be notified to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours of becoming aware of them (Data Protection Act, 2019, s. 43). Report quickly, even if unsure.
